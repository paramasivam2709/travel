Since 2016

From A Chennai Garage To 150+ Destinations

Stackly Adventure began with three friends, one borrowed tent and a wild idea: that world-class adventure travel shouldn't cost a fortune or feel like a package holiday conveyor belt.

Ten years later, we've guided over 50,000 travelers across six continents — summiting at dawn, diving at noon, and dining under stars by night. Yet every itinerary still gets the same obsessive, personal testing that our very first Manali trek received.

  • 50,000+ travelers guided
  • 120 certified local guides
  • Carbon-neutral since 2022
  • 4.9 average traveler rating

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

01

Safety First, Always

No summit is worth a shortcut. Audited gear, certified guides and medical backup are non-negotiable on every trip.

02

Local First

We hire local guides, book family-run lodges and route money into the communities that host us.

03

Leave No Trace

Carbon-neutral operations, trail clean-ups on every trek, and strict wildlife-distance rules.

04

Honest Pricing

The price you see is the price you pay. No surge tricks, no hidden "service fees" at checkout.

05

Small Groups

Twelve travelers maximum. Bigger groups see places; small groups feel them.

06

Relentless Testing

Our team personally hikes, dives and drives every itinerary before it appears on this site.
